## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
FarmConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, and bulk buyers directly.

The platform eliminates unnecessary intermediaries by allowing farmers to list their agricultural products, set fair prices, and sell directly to buyers.

It also provides AI-based demand forecasting, smart logistics, and route optimization to make agricultural distribution faster, cheaper, and more efficient.

Key Features:
- Direct farmer-to-consumer and farmer-to-business connections.
- Online marketplace for agricultural products.
- AI-based crop demand and price prediction.
- Smart transportation and delivery management.
- Real-time product availability and order tracking.
- Multilingual support for farmers.

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility of the Idea
- The platform can be developed using existing web and mobile technologies.
- AI models can use historical agricultural market data to predict demand.
- GPS and mapping services can support transportation and route optimization.
- The system can initially be tested in a small agricultural region before expanding.
Potential Challenges and Risks
1. Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
2. Unstable internet connectivity in rural areas.
3. Difficulty maintaining product quality during transportation.
4. Price fluctuations in agricultural markets.
5. Trust issues between farmers and buyers.
Strategies to Overcome Challenges
- Provide a simple interface with regional language support.
- Introduce SMS and assisted registration facilities.
- Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.
- Provide quality verification and transparent pricing.
- Introduce secure payment and order-tracking systems.
